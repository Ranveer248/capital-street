Getting started

You start in a rooftop studio near Wall Street with $340 and a $2,000 envelope. Rent is $1,850, due at the end of day 3 and every 7 days after. Pay it or you're evicted.

The game clock runs one game minute per real second while the market is open (09:30–16:00), faster at 5× and 20×, and nights fly by. Everything is saved on your device, and to your account if you play online.

A good first week: trade a little on the Share Exchange, take the Starter Loan if you need it and repay it on time, then buy your first property or a cheap plot of land.

Trading

The Share Exchange lists fictional companies: 16 in New York and 4 more in each city you open. Prices move every minute with the market, their sector and their own news. Use market orders to trade now, or limit orders to wait for your price.

Anything can happen: crashes, rallies, buyouts, scandals, hurricanes, blackouts that close the exchange, meme squeezes. Breaking news appears at the top of the screen.

The bank

Big Apple Bank on Wall Street lends from $1,000 up to $500,000. Bigger loans need a better credit score and more net worth. Interest grows every game minute. Repaying on time raises your score. On the due date the bank takes what you owe; if you can't pay, there's a late fee, then a default.

Property

Tap any real building to see its listing: type, floors, price and weekly rent. Buy one floor or the whole building (5% off). Tenants pay rent every night, and prices follow the city's property market.

Deals and auctions

Every morning sellers list some buildings and plots 6–28% below value (they glow gold on the map). Three city auctions open at 09:00 and close at 15:00 the next day (purple). Set a maximum bid and the game bids for you; the winner pays one step above the second-highest maximum.

Land

The open land around each city is cut into 160 m plots. Zoom out to see them outlined. Plots cost about $9,000–$51,000, more near the centre. An empty plot earns a small farmer's lease every night; better, build a business on it.

Factories take what they need from your warehouse every night, in chain order. The factory planner (Property → Business) works out how many mines, wells, mills and works a product needs, what it costs, and how long it takes to pay back.

Logistics

Each city pays its own prices: Tokyo wants ore and steel, Dubai crates, food and machines, London produce and oil, Hong Kong machines and food. Send goods by ship (cheap, about a game day per 6,000 km, needs a port in the city you ship from) or air freight (fast and dear). They sell when they arrive. Truck fleets collect from your farms, mines and wells every night for $40 each.

Inside buildings

Every building you own has two rooms a floor, let to tenants. A Nice fit-out adds 15% rent and Luxury adds 35%. A year lasts 28 days: without heating, winter empties rooms, and without cooling, so does a heatwave. Dig a parking basement, or add up to three floors on top.

Your office

Your first office floor becomes your office. Look inside (the briefcase button), furnish it, and hire interns, employees and brokers. Comfort raises the vibe; server racks and hot days make heat, and air conditioners take it away. A gloomy office makes people quit.

Your company

At a net worth of $250,000, found a company with a name and a ticker. Its value is your empire. Take it public by selling 20–40% to investors for cash; it then trades on the Share Exchange every minute, and its price follows how your empire grows.

Playing online

Press O in the menu, or T in the game, and play as a guest or with an email. You share one world with everyone: other players' buildings and land show in blue with their names, and there's a world chat and one per city. Your game is saved to your account.

Trading with players: list a building or plot at your price or auction it (the server keeps everyone's maximum bid secret), and trade goods on the player exchange, where players set every price. Money and goods reach you even while you're away.

Levels

Almost everything earns XP: trades, rent paid, property, land, businesses, auctions, new cities and each trading day. XP to the next level is 40 × level1.8. Each level gives a new title (Student → Wall Street Legend) and a small cash bonus.
