Capital Street Changelog

Weather you can see

7 November 2026
  • Thunder no longer turns the screen white. Lightning is now a bright jagged bolt with branches and a glow where it lands, in the picture, flickering twice. The sky only lifts a little.
  • Rain you can see: streaks falling at every zoom, rings where the drops land, dark glossy streets, and puddles that fill while it rains, ripple, and dry up afterwards.
  • Storms are darker and greyer. Choose the weather in Settings, or leave it on Auto.

Plots on the Southern Isle

7 November 2026
  • 22 new plots on the Southern Isle: town plots in Belle Rive and Pointe Filaos, farmland on the cane plain, villa plots at Anse Perle, stone ground under Mont Brumeux, a yard by the sugar mill, a quay and a tower plot. Buy, build, rent, renovate and mine them like the others.
  • They work online and offline: reload the game to see them.

Supermarkets

7 November 2026
  • Two supermarkets with a big sign, a car park with parked cars, a trolley corral and a delivery lorry at the side: Capital Fresh Supermarket in the capital, and Soleil Supermarket in Belle Rive on the Southern Isle. People walk to them.

The Southern Isle: the map is now 10 times bigger

7 November 2026
  • A new big island to the south, reached by a long bridge from the Container Terminal. It has Belle Rive (a market town of pastel houses round a square), the Plaine des Cannes (sugar cane in every stage of growth) with the Moulin Doré sugar mill, Pointe Filaos (a fishing village with a pier), Sainte-Aurore at the foot of Mont Brumeux, and Anse Perle (hotels and a long beach on the lagoon bay).
  • Eleven more small islands with their own lagoons and reefs, and a shore of beaches, rocky points and mountains.
  • All together the land is 10 times the first island. Every new place is in the Map panel, under Southern Isle.
  • The trees now grow in a moment after you start, so the island opens faster.
  • Plots and jobs on the Southern Isle come next.

A tropical sea, and weather you can choose

7 November 2026
  • A real tropical sea. A bug had left the water one flat turquoise. Now it has a pale lagoon over the sand, a line of surf where the reef breaks, and deep blue beyond, around every island.
  • Weather you choose (Settings → Weather): Auto, Sunny, Rain, Storm or Tornado. Pick Tornado and the camera flies out to watch it over the sea.
  • Thunder and rain sounds (Settings → Sound): a rumble that arrives after each flash, and the hiss of rain. On a phone the sound starts after your first touch.

The island loads much faster

7 November 2026
  • A progress bar while the island builds. The PLAY button now fills up and names the step (shaping the land, laying the roads, building the towns, planting the trees), instead of a frozen screen.
  • Quicker to start. On a fast PC the island now builds in about 6 seconds instead of 13. On a slow phone it used to take over a minute with the screen stuck; it is now about half a minute, and the detailed ground draws in by itself a moment after you start playing.
  • Phones start on Medium graphics (an iPhone used to be given High, which is heavy). You can still change it on the start screen.
  • The island looks and plays exactly as before: the same ground, the same 38 plots, the same buildings.

Updates arrive by themselves

7 November 2026
  • When a new version is out, the game says "Updating to the newest version…" and loads it: at once on the start screen, or while you play the next time no panel is open, and you go straight back in.
  • The PC and phone apps now open Capital Island, like the website.

Plots online, auctions and island traders

7 November 2026
  • Plots work online. The island server now keeps plots, buildings, resources and income, so other players see what you build (their plots have blue borders) and nobody can own the same plot twice.
  • Auction house (Trade → Auctions): put stone, coal, oil, goods or a whole plot up for 5, 15 or 60 minutes with a starting price and an optional buy-now price. Bids are held until you are outbid; the house keeps 5%. If nobody bids, an island trader buys it at a fair price.
  • Island traders (Trade → Resources): three offers every five minutes to buy from you above the market or sell to you below it. Trading needs an Office or a Tower.
  • Look inside shops and offices: shelves, a counter and a fridge; desks with screens and a meeting table. Offices are now their own building.
  • Roundabouts with a palm and flowers at three open junctions; cars go round them.
  • Six new skyscrapers around the capital, 19 to 27 floors.
  • More detailed cars (door seams, handles, number plates, wheel arches, pillars) and a new low sports car in traffic.
  • Three harbour plots: Eastgate docks, Works quay and South quay.

Plots, business and weather

7 November 2026
  • Buy a plot and build on it. Build & property → Plots, or tap a yellow border on the map. Pull down an old house, then build a house, shop, office, villa, farm, mine, warehouse, factory, harbour or a glass tower. Renovate up to three levels, rent a house out, choose colours and a modern, cosy or luxury interior, and look inside with the roof off.
  • Mine and farm. Mines dig stone, coal or oil; farms grow crates for your food business; a factory turns stone, coal and oil into goods.
  • Jobs need what you own. Jobs → My business has mine shifts, harvest runs, counter shifts and freight runs; each needs the matching building. Trade → Resources sells what you mine, but only if you own an Office or Tower; a Harbour adds 35% on exports.
  • Weather: rain that comes and goes, thunder and lightning in storms, and now and then a tornado far out over the sea. No snow, it is a tropical island.
  • Traffic: traffic lights at the busy town junctions (cars stop on red), cars a little faster, more cars on the bigger island, and deliveries now drop their crates beside the road.
  • A new app icon and cover picture, and a bottom menu with coloured tiles.

A much bigger island

6 November 2026
  • The island is about four times bigger. The first island is unchanged: your plots, homes and progress are exactly where they were. Around it: a mountain with a lookout tower, wooded hills, rivers, a waterfall with a viewing deck, a suspension bridge across Silver Bay, and a second island across the strait.
  • New places to visit: Millford, Northhaven and Eastgate towns, Hillcrest village, Coral Bay, a resort with four hotels on a long beach, a luxury coast and yacht marina, farms, a works estate, a container terminal, an airport with planes, a quarry and a mine.
  • Landmarks: Fort Verity, Lantern Head lighthouse, Silverfall, Sentinel Peak, the Botanical Garden and the Beacon tower.
  • The Map lists every new place by group, with a first-visit bonus. More traffic drives the new roads, including trucks that carry stone and ore to the works estate.
  • Add ?classic to the address to play on the first island only.

A working island

6 November 2026
  • Contracts and Wholesale now work online. Jobs → Contracts: businesses pay you to carry their cargo and you pay nothing. Trade → Wholesale: buy goods and fuel at the Harbour depot and send them to the shops, the hotel, the co-op or the plant. Prices move as the shelves empty and fill.
  • Capital Sports Park between the Business Centre and the stadium: an athletics track round a pitch, a basketball court, a tennis court, a pavilion and tree-lined paths, with a fan plaza of ticket booths and flags at the stadium gate.
  • Market Square now has shops on two sides (a bakery, a florist, a grocer and more), and people walk to them.
  • Industrial Isle is a working site: a works gate on the bridge road, a tank farm with a pipe rack to the plant, a workshop, a container yard and a wharf with a crane where the cargo boat ties up.
  • Greenfields has meadows with wildflowers and hay bales and a pond; the ground is greener or drier by place, and the hills are wooded.
  • Office towers have their own tops (masts, stepped crowns, roof gardens, glass pyramids) and shop awnings at street level.
  • The title screen no longer says Preview, and there is a What's new link in Settings.

Capital Island is the game

5 November 2026
  • The main address opens Capital Island; the New York game is at ?newyork.
  • Connected streets and footpaths, working farms and districts, six house styles, vehicles with turning wheels and people on the pavements.
  • The game checks your graphics before you play and lets you pick Auto, Low, Medium or High.
  • Money in and out on Home shows the last ten minutes, and what each change was for.

A real start

31 October 2026

  • Penny's first mission: buy a share, sell it, collect $500, then pick your goal. Time stops while she talks.
  • The loading screen shows each step, lets you pick graphics quality, and explains what to do if your browser can't show 3D.
  • Your save in Settings: see when it last saved, get warned if it can't, and download a backup.
  • Multiplayer: invite a friend with a link, find players' buildings, a clear online status that reconnects by itself, and safer trades checked by the server.
