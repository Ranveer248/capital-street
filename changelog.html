Capital Street Changelog

Streets, roofs and cream panels

31 October 2026
  • Street lamps, benches and street trees along the pavements.
  • Tiled terracotta roofs on many more homes, and balconies on blocks of flats.
  • All the menus are now a clean cream style.

An illustrated city

31 October 2026
  • The whole city is now drawn with ink outlines, like an illustration.
  • Warmer colours, darker roads with crisp markings, window sills and painted shutters.
  • Less traffic jamming the junctions.

Daily tasks and colourful roofs

31 October 2026
  • Daily tasks: three new tasks every day in Jobs. Finish them for cash, XP and a bonus.
  • Buildings have colourful roofs and crisper windows.

A brand-new screen

31 October 2026
  • New controls: Exchange and Property bottom-left, a big JOBS button in the corner, and Home, Bank, Office and World up the right.
  • A new lobby over the city, with today's deals and auctions one tap away.
  • Chunky, tactile buttons and a new game font.
  • A flatter city-builder camera.

Build your own New York

30 October 2026
  • A bright new look: a sunny, toy-like city that runs smoothly on phones.
  • Rebuild any building you own: knock it down and build a cottage, shop, apartments, offices or a skyscraper in your own colour.
  • The new JOBS button: twelve ways to make money, all paying about the same.
  • All-new icons, and Penny's tutorial shows you the new things.
  • Capital Street is now online only.

Easier to play, a livelier city

30 October 2026
  • The same button bar in the game and on the main menu: tap Exchange, Property or any other button on the menu to go straight there.
  • A gold EARN button shows every way to make money in one place.
  • Easier trading: pick an amount and tap BUY or SELL ALL.
  • Fewer, smaller market crashes, and more good news.
  • Cars with headlights at night, ferries and boats on the rivers, helicopters, and more planes.
  • The slow-device warning is gone.

Stunning graphics, a new menu and a tutorial

30 October 2026
  • All-new graphics: glowing lights, film-like colours, drifting clouds, glass towers that reflect the sky, real waves on the water, and a sun that follows the clock from morning to golden evening to a city of lit windows at night.
  • A new main menu with one big Play button, and the game is now online: pick a name and play in the same city as everyone else.
  • A cleaner screen and map: only your apartment and buildings show until you go shopping for property.
  • Penny the piggy bank teaches new players how to play.
  • More surprises: bull runs, flash crashes, bubbles, bank runs and more. Planes fly over the city, and a rare crash can even hit your building.

Capital Street on PC

30 October 2026
  • A desktop version for Windows, Mac and Linux that plays offline and online, with F11 for full screen.

Logistics

30 October 2026
  • Ports on waterfront plots, with a crane and stacks of containers.
  • Ships and air freight carry your goods to your other cities, each with its own prices. Ships sail out of your port on the map.
  • Truck fleets collect from your farms, mines and wells every night.

Your own company

30 October 2026
  • Found a company at $250,000 net worth and take it public: its ticker trades on the Share Exchange and follows your empire.

Inside buildings

30 October 2026
  • Tenants, fit-outs, heating and cooling, parking basements and extra floors.
  • A 28-day year with winters and heatwaves; the office now has air conditioners, and server racks make heat.

Markets and trading with players

30 October 2026
  • Factories: food factory, steel mill, refinery and machine works, plus a factory planner.
  • Sell goods at your own price.
  • Online: a player exchange where players set every price, and selling or auctioning buildings and plots to other players.

Businesses

30 October 2026
  • Build farms, mines, oil wells and shops on your land; collect, upgrade and sell to the city.

Land and a property market

30 October 2026
  • Land plots around every city, daily deals, auctions with max bids, and everything for sale lit up on the map.

Bright and smooth, with levels

30 October 2026
  • A vivid new look, springy animations, smooth zoom and brighter daylight.
  • Levels and XP with a level-up screen.

Multiplayer and cloud saves

29 October 2026
  • Play online as a guest or with an email: one shared world, chat, and cloud saves that follow your account.
  • The Android and iPhone apps build automatically.

The first ten goals

Before 29 October 2026
  • Real New York, then London, Tokyo, Hong Kong and Dubai, with buildings that look like where they're built.
  • Trading, the bank, buying property, your office and staff, a PC-first menu and controls.
